## 📝 Full Description

**TURN YOUR LIVING ROOM INTO AN ARCADE RECYCLING CENTER!** ♻️✨

Welcome to **Recycle Rush MR**, a next-generation Mixed Reality (MR) arcade simulator built exclusively for Meta Quest! Say goodbye to virtual boundaries and joystick movement. Thanks to advanced Meta Passthrough technology, the game takes place entirely inside your physical room. 

Watch in awe as magical spatial portals open on your ceiling, dropping recyclable waste (Paper, Glass, and Plastic) directly onto your actual floor. Your mission? Physically walk around your room, grab the falling trash, and toss them into the correct holographic recycling bins to build massive combos, earn XP, and unlock achievements!

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES 🌟

🔥 **True Mixed Reality (MR) Experience**
No motion sickness, no virtual friction. See your real-world environment while interacting with high-fidelity holographic bins and dynamic portals. Move freely and safely in your own space.

📈 **Deep Progression & 15 Thrilling Levels**
Master the art of sorting across 15 carefully balanced levels. As you progress, portals drop items faster, challenging missions appear, and the game pushes your reflexes to the limit. Can you reach the final "Arcade Master" level?

⚡ **Dynamic Economy & Golden Waste**
Earn XP and Coins for every correct throw. Build up to a x5 Combo Multiplier for massive score boosts. Keep your eyes peeled for the rare **Golden Waste**—a shimmering treasure that grants massive rewards if caught in time!

🎲 **6 Exciting Random Events**
Expect the unexpected! The game dynamically injects random events to test your skills:
*   ⏱️ **Slow Motion:** Matrix-style sorting for 10 seconds.
*   🚀 **Speed Mode:** Portals go into overdrive!
*   🍀 **Lucky Drop:** Increased Golden Waste spawns!
*   ...and more (Double XP, Double Coin, Mega Combo)!

🏆 **Robust Achievement System**
Are you a completionist? Unlock hidden achievements like *Golden Rain*, *Perfect Streak*, and *Cool-Headed Master* to show off your recycling prowess.

🎵 **Adaptive Audio & Spatial Haptics**
Feel every throw with responsive controller haptics. Hear where the trash is falling with 3D Spatial Audio, and feel your adrenaline pump as the background music dynamically speeds up during the final 30 seconds of your run!
